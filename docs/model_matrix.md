## Implementation Guidelines & Parameter Parity Rules

1. **Parameter Bounds & Pathologies:** Enforce non-negativity bounds ($\beta \ge 0$, $\alpha \ge 0$) in `src/models/m1_lq.py` to prevent fitting failures caused by the negative $\beta$ pathology observed in **29.8% of ion curves** and **7.5% of photon curves** in raw PIDE data.
2. **Biphasic Repair Timescales:** Model **M5** partitions DSB repair into two distinct kinetic components: a **fast repair phase** ($t_{1/2} = 10\text{–}15\text{ min}$) and a **slow repair phase** ($t_{1/2} = 1\text{–}2\text{ h}$).
3. **Single-Track Complexity Thresholds:** Model **M4** applies minimum LET cutoffs of **$9.4\text{ keV/}\mu\text{m}$ for protons** and **$11.5\text{ keV/}\mu\text{m}$ for $\alpha$-particles** for single-track clustered damage.
4. **Dosimetric Uncertainty Weighting:** Model **M5** incorporates a specialized likelihood weighting function during Bayesian inference (`PyMC` / `Stan`) to account for up to **75% dosimetric uncertainty** present in short-range high-LET experimental data.
